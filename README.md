# 🎓 Calverse - Pro Level Productivity for Students

> Your central command center for academics, calculations, and deep work.

## About Calverse

**Calverse** is a beautifully designed, all-in-one productivity platform for students and learners. It combines premium UI design with powerful computational tools in a single, seamless web experience.

## Why Calverse?

Students struggle with scattered tools:
- Multiple apps for different calculators
- Lost calculation history
- Untracked study habits
- Disorganized notes
- Wasted time jumping between platforms

**Calverse solves this** — everything you need, in one beautiful interface.

## 🏠 Academia Homepage

## ✨ Features

### 🧮 All 3 Calculator Systems

#### **Math Calculators**
Linear Equations, Matrix Operations, Quadratic Equations, Binomial Expansion, Permutations & Combinations, Inverse Trigonometry, and more.

#### **Physics Calculators**
Kinematic Equations, Force & Motion, Energy & Power, Waves, Thermodynamics, Electromagnetism calculations.

#### **Chemistry Calculators**
Molecular Weight, Molar Mass, pH & pOH, Stoichiometry, Gas Laws, Chemical Bonding analysis.
